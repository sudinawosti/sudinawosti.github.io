Fynex
SaaSE-commerce FinanceMVP Live

Fynex pulls transaction data from Shopify, Stripe, and Amazon APIs, auto-reconciles it across platforms, and generates audit-ready financial reports for e-commerce operators.

The problem. Multi-channel businesses have revenue landing in different places, fees applied differently, refunds and chargebacks recorded inconsistently. By the time a finance lead tries to assemble a clean monthly picture, they're reconciling by hand and still producing reports with gaps.

The solution. A reconciliation engine that normalises transactions into a unified schema, flags cross-platform mismatches automatically, and outputs clean reports with AI-generated explanations alongside every flagged discrepancy.

Polymarket Agent
AI AgentOn-chainLive Dry-run

An AI agent that monitors the on-chain behaviour of top-performing Polymarket wallets, identifies recurring patterns in how they enter and size positions, and logs trades based on detected signals.

Why it exists. Personal project to get hands-on with AI agent engineering, on-chain data analysis, and autonomous decision systems — and to study market microstructure on a live, high-volume venue. Polymarket cleared $21B in volume in 2025.

Current mode. Live dry-run — watching real wallets make real trades, logging paper positions with hypothetical P&L whenever three or more wallets converge on the same market. Not yet deploying real capital.
